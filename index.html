Who We Are

Student-run consulting and education.

SBG provides pro-bono consulting to sustainability-focused companies and to organizations working to bring sustainable practices into their operations. Our clients range from Atlanta startups to Fortune 500 companies.

We also run a Consultant Fellow program and a speaker series, so that students from any major can learn about consulting and careers in sustainability.
