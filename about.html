About SBG

A student-run consulting organization at Emory focused on sustainability.

Our Mission

A home at Emory for students interested in business and sustainability.

Our goal is to create an Emory community for people interested in business and sustainability, and to introduce undergraduate students to important topics and professional opportunities within the field.

SBG is part of the BBA Council, but membership is open to every Emory undergraduate on the Atlanta and Oxford campuses, whatever their major.

What We Do

Two branches: consulting and education.

Consulting

Student teams do pro-bono ESG consulting for companies ranging from startups to the Fortune 500. Most of our teams work directly with businesses in the Atlanta area. You can apply to be consultants or Project Leads.

Consultant Fellow Initiative

A program for any student who wants to learn how consulting works. Fellows learn the basics before joining a client team.

Speaker Series

Talks from people working in sustainability, ESG and consulting, so members can hear first-hand what careers in the field look like.

How a Project Works

One semester, start to finish.

Each team is run by a Project Lead and supported by the executive board and our board of directors.

Scope

We meet with the client to agree on the question, goals and final deliverable.

Research

The team studies the client's industry, competitors and sustainability data.

Build

We draft recommendations and check in with the client along the way.

Present

The team presents its final recommendations to the client.
