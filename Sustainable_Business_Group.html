About Us

The premier sustainable business community at Emory.

Our goal is to create an Emory community for people interested in business and sustainability, and to introduce undergraduate students to important topics and professional opportunities within the field.

SBG empowers all Atlanta and Oxford undergraduates with the knowledge, connections, and experiences necessary to thrive in this evolving field. Although we sit under the BBA Council, we welcome students of every major — from Business to Environmental Sciences and beyond.

Consulting

Pro-bono ESG consulting for startups through Fortune 500 companies that are sustainability-oriented or striving to become more sustainable.

Education

Speaker series, workshops, and our Consultant Fellow Initiative introduce students to sustainability careers and consulting skills.

Community

A network of students, alumni, and a professional board of directors united by a shared commitment to sustainable business.

What We Do

Real projects. Real impact.

We combine hands-on consulting with education so members graduate ready to lead in sustainable business.

ESG Consulting

Student teams deliver pro-bono strategy work on sustainability challenges — from emissions reduction to customer engagement and market positioning.

Consultant Fellow Initiative

Speaker Series

Conversations with professionals working across sustainability, ESG, and corporate strategy, giving members direct insight into the field.

Featured Project

Partnering with Delta Air Lines.

Tackling one of aviation's toughest challenges: engaging customers in more sustainable travel.

Client: Delta Air Lines · Aviation

A sustainability-focused loyalty framework

Delta asked SBG to explore how it could better engage its customers around sustainability. Rather than simply offering ideas, our team built a concrete, actionable solution aligned with Delta's existing customer engagement strategy.

  • Designed a conceptual loyalty program, modeled on Delta's current programs, that rewards climate-conscious travel.
  • Balanced customer engagement with environmental impact to help reduce Scope 1 emissions.
  • Presented final recommendations at Delta's headquarters to the Chief Sustainability Officer and the sustainability and loyalty teams.
