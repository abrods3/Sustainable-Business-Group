Home Depot: Induction Cooking Consumer Education Campaign

Develop a strategy to educate Home Depot customers on induction cooking — including how the technology actually works, addressing adoption barriers, surfacing available rebates, and positioning Home Depot as the trusted destination for customers making the switch.

  • Build a mainstream messaging strategy for induction ranges that addresses key adoption barriers and translates rebates, energy savings, and carbon reduction into clear customer benefits.
  • Deliverables: a customer barrier map, a rebate landscape across key Home Depot markets, customer-facing energy and carbon savings figures, and a consumer education campaign that explains how induction works.
  • Project Lead: Jack Maney

PSC: Solar Plus Storage Pilot Business Case

Develop a business case that quantifies the financial opportunity for Georgia hospitals to adopt solar and battery storage through Georgia Power's Solar Plus Storage Pilot, comparing customer-directed and utility-directed models with Boston Medical Center's Clean Power Prescription Program, to support hospital adoption decisions and demonstrate the pilot's viability for expansion.

  • Building the financial case for Atlanta hospitals (Emory and CHOA) to join Georgia Power's Solar Plus Storage Pilot, modeling both customer- and utility-directed pathways and benchmarking against Boston Medical Center's program.
  • Deliverables: Financial models with sensitivity analyses, a comparative business case report, an executive summary of key metrics (NPV, payback, savings, carbon reduction), and a pitch deck for hospital leadership.
  • Project Lead: Ansthrutha Shrinivas

ECA Solar: BESS Go-To-Market Strategy

Outline a Go-To-Market strategy for ECA, including addressing questions such as: How much budget to assign to ECA’s BESS efforts, How to staff the company appropriately, How to design ECA’s corporate organizational structure to not over-expose ECA’s core community solar business

  • Evaluating whether and how ECA should expand from community solar into front-of-the-meter battery storage in Illinois, weighing standalone BESS revenue potential against adding storage to solar projects.
  • Deliverables: A BESS value proposition and revenue stack analysis, a solar-only vs. solar-plus-storage tradeoff assessment, and a go-to-market plan covering budget, staffing, and an org structure that protects the core community solar business.
  • Project Lead: Nathaniel Green

Better Earth: Impact Calculator Strategy

Take Better Earth’s beta Plastic Impact Calculator from an internal Excel-based model to a credible, scalable, customer-facing software solution that helps customers understand the sustainability impact of their purchasing decisions.

  • Helping Better Earth turn its Excel-based sustainability impact calculator into a credible, customer-ready software product by validating the methodology, defining what customers need, and identifying where AI can streamline the workflow.
  • Deliverables: A validated methodology, a customer needs and metrics assessment, an end-to-end customer workflow design, an AI and automation opportunity assessment, a positioning and messaging framework, and a roadmap from beta to functional prototype.
  • Project Lead: Avaneesh Reddy

Super South: Capstone Challenge Strategy

Develop a strategic framework to launch and promote Super South's Capstone Challenge as a premier showcase for student sustainability innovation projects across the Southeast.

  • Designing the launch of a student Capstone Challenge, from recruiting participating universities and student groups to defining how submissions are structured and judged.
  • Deliverables: A submission framework and evaluation criteria, a pre-launch promotion strategy, a post-launch recruitment and engagement roadmap, and a finalist communication timeline.
  • Project Lead: Jackson Wentz
