Delta Air Lines: Sustainable Travel Loyalty Framework

Delta asked our team to look at how the airline could better engage its customers around sustainability. The team designed a concept for a loyalty framework that rewards more sustainable travel choices, modeled on the structure of Delta's existing loyalty programs.

  • Aimed to encourage climate-conscious travel and help reduce Delta's Scope 1 emissions.
  • Built to fit Delta's existing customer engagement strategy.
  • Presented at Delta's Atlanta headquarters to the Chief Sustainability Officer and members of the sustainability and loyalty teams.
