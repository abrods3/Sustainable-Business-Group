Home Depot: Induction Cooking Consumer Education Campaign

Energy

PSC: Solar Plus Storage Pilot Business Case

Develop a business case that quantifies the financial opportunity for Georgia hospitals to adopt solar and battery storage through Georgia Power's Solar Plus Storage Pilot, comparing customer-directed and utility-directed models with Boston Medical Center's Clean Power Prescription Program, to support hospital adoption decisions and demonstrate the pilot's viability for expansion.

  • Building the financial case for Atlanta hospitals (Emory and CHOA) to join Georgia Power's Solar Plus Storage Pilot, modeling both customer- and utility-directed pathways and benchmarking against Boston Medical Center's program.
  • Deliverables: Financial models with sensitivity analyses, a comparative business case report, an executive summary of key metrics (NPV, payback, savings, carbon reduction), and a pitch deck for hospital leadership.
  • Project Lead: Ansthrutha Shrinivas
Energy

ECA Solar: BESS Go-To-Market Strategy

Outline a Go-To-Market strategy for ECA, including addressing questions such as: How much budget to assign to ECA’s BESS efforts, How to staff the company appropriately, How to design ECA’s corporate organizational structure to not over-expose ECA’s core community solar business

  • Evaluating whether and how ECA should expand from community solar into front-of-the-meter battery storage in Illinois, weighing standalone BESS revenue potential against adding storage to solar projects.
  • Deliverables: A BESS value proposition and revenue stack analysis, a solar-only vs. solar-plus-storage tradeoff assessment, and a go-to-market plan covering budget, staffing, and an org structure that protects the core community solar business.
  • Project Lead: Nathaniel Green
