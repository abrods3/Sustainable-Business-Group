Ways to Get Involved

Start wherever fits you.

Many members start by coming to events or joining the Consultant Fellow Initiative, then move onto a client team. After a semester as a consultant, you can apply to be a Project Lead.

  • General MemberCome to the speaker series and general body meetings.
  • Consultant FellowLearn the basics of consulting through our training program.
  • ConsultantJoin a team working on a project for a real client.
  • Project LeadRun a client team. Open to members who have completed a semester as a consultant.
